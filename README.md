## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between single wall and double wall corrugated sheets?

Single wall sheets contain one fluted layer, while double wall sheets contain two. The additional fluted layer generally provides greater thickness and structural support, depending on the board grade.

### Can double wall sheets be used for heavy products?

Yes, they can be used for many heavier packaging applications. However, the appropriate board grade depends on product weight, stacking conditions, and the type of protection required.

### Are double wall sheets suitable for pallet layer pads?

Yes. They can be placed between layers of palletized products to help create a more even surface and separate goods.

### Can corrugated sheets be customized to specific dimensions?

Corrugated sheets can generally be cut or converted into different sizes, depending on the material specification and manufacturing capabilities.

### Are these sheets recyclable?

Clean paper-based corrugated sheets are generally recyclable where local facilities accept them. Coatings, contamination, and other material components may affect recycling eligibility.
